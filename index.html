Questions

Do I have to conduct the technical demo?

No. We run every demo and answer technical questions. Associates are welcome to join.

What exactly does a Partnerships Associate do?

Finds and approaches suitable business owners, qualifies them, arranges a demo with us, follows up, and helps move qualified prospects to a signed agreement. We handle the demo, proposal support, delivery and support.

What exactly does a Referral Partner do?

Gets a business owner's permission and introduces them to us. Nothing else is required. We take it from there.

Which role should I choose?

Choose Partnerships Associate if you want to sell actively and earn more per client. Choose Referral Partner if you only want to make occasional introductions.

Do I have to pay any joining fee?

No. There's no joining fee for either role.

Are travel or outreach expenses reimbursed?

No. Both roles are commission-only, so expenses are your own. Please weigh this before applying.

How and when is commission paid?

On the 10th of the month after we receive the relevant client payment, by bank transfer or UPI, once we have your PAN and bank details.

What happens if a client stops paying?

You keep what you've already been paid (except where a payment is refunded, as set out in the agreement). No further commission or milestones are earned on payments the client doesn't make.

How do I register an introduction, and what if two people introduce the same business?
  • Register it by WhatsApp or email with the business name, area, owner's name and confirmation that the owner agreed to be contacted.
  • We confirm in writing within 7 days whether it's accepted.
  • The first introduction we accept gets the credit. Our written acceptance, with its date and time, is the record used to settle any dispute.
  • A business that's already our client, or that we've been in contact with in the last 180 days, isn't eligible. We'll tell you when you register it.
  • A business we haven't been in contact with for 180 days or more can be registered again, and the new accepted introduction counts.
How long does an accepted introduction remain valid?

If the business becomes a client within 180 days of our acceptance, it counts as yours. How ongoing negotiations at the end of that period are treated is set out in the written agreement.

Can I work with other companies?

Yes, there's no exclusivity. Please don't sell a directly competing automation service to the same client at the same time.

Can I negotiate prices or promise extra features?

No. Please use our price list and written scope only. Any change to price or features has to come from QuickPort AI in writing. Never promise results such as more bookings or revenue.

What happens to commission after the first 12 months?

Associate commission covers the setup fee and the first 12 monthly payments. Referral milestones end at the 6th monthly payment. Later payments don't earn commission unless agreed in writing.

What happens when a partnership ends?

Either side can end it with 30 days' notice. For clients you brought in before it ended, you continue to receive commission on their eligible payments as they are actually collected, until that client's commission period ends.

Is a written agreement provided?

Yes. Shortlisted partners receive a simple one-page agreement covering payment triggers, refunds, attribution, confidentiality, data handling and termination.

How will you protect my information and that of businesses I introduce?

Your application details are used only to evaluate your application and contact you. Details of businesses you introduce are used only to contact them about QuickPort AI, with their permission, and are never shared or sold. We ask you to treat our client and pricing information as confidential too.

How long does a client usually take to decide?

We don't have enough history yet to give an average. We'll keep you updated on every introduction.
